What GramSahay Does

One platform. Every civic problem.

From a broken road to a pending government scheme — GramSahay gives every rural citizen a voice, a reference number, and a resolution.

📝
Register Any Civic Issue
Roads, water supply, pensions, health cards, housing schemes — citizens report any problem in minutes via WhatsApp, in their own language.
🎫
Instant Ticket Number
Every issue gets a unique ticket ID the moment it's submitted. No more verbal complaints that disappear — every request is recorded and traceable.
🔔
Right Person Notified
The assigned community coordinator gets an instant WhatsApp alert with full issue details — no delays, no middlemen, no lost paperwork.
📍
Village & Mauja Precision
Issues are routed down to the exact mauja (hamlet) level — ensuring the right coordinator handles the right locality every single time.
🔄
Live Status Updates
Citizens receive automatic WhatsApp updates as their issue moves from Pending → In Progress → Resolved. No follow-up calls needed.
👥
Report on Behalf
One person can report for an elderly neighbour, a family member, or anyone in the village — bridging the digital gap for those who need it most.
